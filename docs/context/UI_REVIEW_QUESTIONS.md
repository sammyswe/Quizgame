# UI review questions

Use these after viewing the current build. Answer individually; each answer becomes either a
LOCKED direction, a scoped iteration, or an explicitly deferred idea.

## Fleet question scene

1. Does the three-quarter side-view camera show enough of the fleet?
2. Are ships large enough to feel like the main characters?
3. Is the captain sufficiently obvious without dominating the board?
4. Are nickname hull plates readable?
5. Do fleet positions communicate rank clearly?
6. Are the four islands visually distinct enough?
7. Is answer text readable without making islands feel like cards?
8. Is the sky-banner question too large, too small, or correctly weighted?
9. Does the chest-and-falling-coins timer communicate urgency?
10. Is the side leaderboard the right size?
11. Is the bottom HUD compact enough?
12. Are five inventory slots appropriate?
13. Does selecting then confirming an island feel decisive?

## Reveal

14. Do hidden choices remain sufficiently secret before reveal?
15. Is ship travel fast enough?
16. Does travel order make the speed reward understandable?
17. Is correct treasure collection satisfying without being excessive?
18. Is the wrong-answer treatment clear enough?
19. Are concise event cards useful or intrusive?
20. Is one event at a time paced correctly?
21. Is the leaderboard pause after every question welcome or too disruptive?

## Mutiny and marooning

22. Is the black-flag control discoverable?
23. Is two-press confirmation sufficient?
24. Does secret cannon preparation communicate commitment privately?
25. Does partial mutiny look too punishing or not punishing enough?
26. Does unanimous cannon fire feel coordinated?
27. Is the captain's evade/tax distinction clear?
28. Is the maroon crash funny enough?
29. Does the private castaway scene feel meaningfully different?
30. Is the island chest consolation too generous, too weak, or appropriate?

## Items

31. Can ownership be understood from visible ship item markers?
32. Can active effects be distinguished from unused inventory?
33. Is drag/tap targeting intuitive?
34. Are invalid-target explanations sufficiently clear?
35. Which item animation currently feels weakest?
36. Does any animation obscure the shared board too much?
37. Are cannonball and barnacle distortions difficult in the intended way?
38. Is the five-second pre-answer item window still desired after seeing the flow?

## Loot Drop

39. Does the cabin treasure-map scene feel like a bigger expedition?
40. Is coin dragging accurate and fast enough?
41. Are MAX controls useful or too form-like?
42. Is one ship per roughly 50 gold the right visual scale?
43. Do venture destinations need labels or stronger environmental identity?
44. Is the 5–10 second reveal target correct?
45. Which wrong-venture destruction should be produced first?

## Wildcards and rewards

46. Does Poseidon feel noble and surprising?
47. Is the delay before rescue long enough to register the apparent loss?
48. Should the fish-guided reroute be more comic or more majestic?
49. Are sharks funny enough and non-frightening?
50. Does the percentage loss read as a bounded bite rather than total failure?
51. Is Shark Tooth the right visual/name?
52. What should Shark Tooth actually do?
53. Does the chest ceremony feel exciting without becoming the game's dominant identity?
54. Are rarity differences clear enough?
55. Is the opening too long, too short, or correct?

## Shell and overall identity

56. Does the lobby feel like a ship deck rather than a menu?
57. Is avatar selection prominent enough?
58. Does “The Seven Seas” intro create the right opening mood?
59. Does the shell still contain too much neon?
60. Does the winner scene need more world animation?
61. Which existing fallback asset should be replaced first once Higgsfield is available?
62. Does the build now feel like an arcade pirate game rather than a quiz website?

## Things to specifically watch

1. Does the most knowledgeable player win ~3 in 4 games?
2. Do players actually talk during questions, or silently tap answers?
3. Is the False Map round producing lies and accusations?
4. Does anyone ever choose Guard in Split or Plunder? (If never: buff the counter-bonus.)
5. Are legendary items exciting without feeling unfair to the leader?
6. Is the reveal queue pacing right (too slow = phones out, too fast = confusion)?
7. Do trailing players feel the comeback (underdog/sunken chests, final actions)?
8. Is 45s per question too long/short with real discussion?
